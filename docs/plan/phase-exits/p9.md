## Honesty notes

- **The ADR-25 verdict does not exist yet, by design.** The gate needs ≥10k
  logged decisions or the 60-day sunset; the log shipped dark in v0.3.0 and
  the deployment enables it at v0.4.0 (operator action, this exit). Until
  the dwell concludes, ε-greedy remains the routing policy and
  `GET /v1/decision-log/ope` reports `insufficient_data` — no silent limbo:
  the verdict lands in this file's addendum or the v0.5.0 exit, whichever
  comes first.
- **Suite 15 falsified its own ADR twice before passing** (recorded in
  ADR-26): novelty-only value estimates chased deep-ranked chaff, and
  Pandora's stopping rule answers the single-best-find question while page
  ranking is additive. The shipped selector is the additive-objective
  greedy; `pandora_walk` stays for a future answer mode. A benchmark that
  cannot falsify the design is decoration — this one earned its gate.
- **The VoI value model ships without its embedding-coverage term.** The
  hermetic replay has no embedding space to calibrate it; bolting on an
  uncalibrated term would un-freeze suite-validated constants. It joins
  when a calibratable signal study exists (carried forward).
- **The ingest frontier is not VoI-ordered** — ADR-26 sketched it, but
  today's ingest is operator-push with no in-process queue to reorder.
  Recorded as a deviation rather than inventing a queue nobody operates.
- **MC propensities under the contextual policy are approximate** (64
  posterior draws, floored at 1/64) where ε-greedy's are exact. DR is
  robust to moderate propensity error; the v0.4.0+ gate run re-validates
  against logged MC propensities before any uplift number is trusted.

## Deviations from plan

1. **Pandora → additive selector** (ADR-26 amendment, measured — see honesty
   notes).
2. **OPE core lives in meridian-searx, not meridian-eval** (re-exported):
   the API computes the gate report in-process next to the log it reads.
3. **WBS 9.3's "offline bridge to train/" was not built** — the gate runs
   in-process via the endpoint; a Python training bridge has no consumer
   until the linear-TS policy needs offline retraining (Phase 10 at the
   earliest).
4. **fetch_budget is mutually exclusive with compare** (not in the original
   sketch): a compare's anon half must never inherit a fetch budget.
