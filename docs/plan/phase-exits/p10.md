## Honesty notes

- **The answer-mode latency row was missed and re-derived, not absorbed.**
  The 3.0s figure was an a-priori planning guess; the measurement (3224ms
  p50 at fetch_budget=2) reflects the per-fetch CE realization that makes
  the Pandora stop honest — the exact mechanism the suite validated. The
  budgets doc re-issues the row at ≤3.5s measured-based; "reduce per-doc
  passage count" is the carried optimization study (untested knobs don't get
  flipped at exit time). Risk #8's tripwire text prescribed exactly this
  path at Phase 0.
- **The first device probe ran against a degraded engine pool** (deep
  baseline read 2506ms; several `searx_unavailable`). The recorded gotcha —
  engine suspensions compound in sidecar memory; restart clears — fixed it;
  the clean re-probe (2173ms, 16/16 ok) is the gate measurement and BOTH
  runs are recorded.
- **Two features died by their own gates this phase** (bands pre-ship,
  embedding-coverage pre-amendment). Neither was "wasted work": suite 16 is
  the standing judge for any stronger confidence predictor, suite 15b for
  any future use of the (real, measured) embedding signal — named candidate:
  answer-mode pruning, where redundant copies have no reveal reward.
- **A truncated merge output caused committed conflict markers** on the 15b
  branch (caught by CI fmt, fixed same hour). Recorded because process
  lessons are lessons: full merge output gets read, not tail -3.
- **The deployed scratch/musl appliance degrades answer mode honestly**
  (`fetch_unavailable` — no CE in that image), exactly as it does deep
  rerank and fetch_budget; the gnu/ort deep image carries the full path.
  Device validation ran the deep binary on the host against the live
  sidecar, as at the P9 exit.
