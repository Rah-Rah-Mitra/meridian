# Suite 15b `voi-embed` — the embedding-coverage study (2026-06-13, Pi 5)

**Verdict: AMEND-OR-RECORD → RECORD. The P9 carry ("embedding-coverage term
for the VoI value model, with a calibration study") closes with a measured
no.** No production change. Machine-readable run:
`2026-06-13-pi5-p10-voi-embed.json`.

## The two findings, separated

1. **The signal exists.** Calibration sanity: mean potion cosine between two
   REGISTERS of the same real-word semantic field (e.g. "volcano eruption
   lava magma…" vs "volcanic explosion molten rock plume…") is **0.716**;
   between different fields **0.052**. The embedding space cleanly sees the
   paraphrase redundancy that MinHash containment cannot (low exact-token
   overlap → sketch novelty stays HIGH on a paraphrased copy). The
   motivating case is real and measurable.

2. **No swept value model spends the signal profitably.** On a replay corpus
   where each decisive original has one token copy AND one paraphrase copy
   (the frozen v0.4.0 model wastes fetches on the latter — 8.8 of 10 budget
   vs suite-15's 6.95 on token-only copies):

   | Combiner (gain discount) | tuning nDCG@10 | vs fetch-all | fetches |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | min(novelty, coverage) | 0.6964 | −0.043 | 5.17 |
   | novelty × coverage | 0.6963 | −0.043 | 5.15 |
   | (novelty + coverage)/2 | 0.7244 | −0.015 | 7.90 |
   | frozen (novelty only) | — reference — | −0.0 within gate | 8.79 |

   Every combiner saves fetches (up to 41%) but breaches the ±0.01 quality
   bar: **in the page-reveal value model, fetching a paraphrase copy still
   buys nDCG** — it confirms a relevant doc and moves it up — so pricing
   paraphrases out trades real page quality for the saved fetch. The
   discount is too blunt; the verdict is recorded, not nudged.

## Why this closes the carry honestly

The ADR-26 deviation said the term "joins when a calibratable signal study
exists". The study now exists: the signal is calibratable (finding 1), and
the straightforward family of gain discounts is measurably unprofitable
(finding 2). A future value model that uses the embedding signal
differently — e.g. answer-mode candidate pruning (single-best regime has no
page-reveal reward for redundant copies), or a learned tradeoff — has this
suite as its standing judge; `voi-embed` skips (never passes) without
`models/`.
