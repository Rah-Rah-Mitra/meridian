## Suite-12 same-lane JSD noise floor (Phase-8 entry evidence)

12 region-sensitive queries × 8 repeats per lane, limit-busted caches,
2.5s/query pacing, ANALYZE_PREFIX=20:

| Lane | pairs | mean | CI95 | p50 | p90 | empty runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| direct (bandit-driven) | 281 | 0.294 | [0.258, 0.333] | 0.05 | **0.667** | 11/96 |
| anon (instance-default engines) | 336 | 0.096 | [0.082, 0.110] | 0.052 | **0.300** | 0/96 |

**Findings that reshaped Phase 8:**
1. **The direct lane's within-lane churn is dominated by the ε-greedy bandit**,
   not by engine result drift: repeats ride different engine-subset arms
   (p90 JSD 0.667 vs anon's 0.300 on fixed engines). A vantage comparison
   against bandit churn would be meaningless ⇒ `compare=vantages` pins BOTH
   halves to instance-default engines (`pin_engines`), making the halves
   differ by vantage only. The Phase-8 cross-lane gate references the
   fixed-engine floor: **p90 0.30** (`search.compare_noise_floor_p90`).
2. **The 800ms direct metasearch deadline was killing real fan-outs** on this
   network (engines need 0.8–1.6s; first probe run: 90/96 direct responses
   empty). Operator override 2500ms applied; 11/96 still empty at 2500ms —
   engines are simply flaky; the probe now reports `empty_runs` explicitly.
3. Earlier invalid runs (kept for the record in the exit note): 300ms pacing
   rate-limited the engines themselves — probe pacing is now 2.5s/query on
   both lanes.
