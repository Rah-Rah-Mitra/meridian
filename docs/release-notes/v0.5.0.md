# Meridian v0.5.0 — the honest-verdicts release

Phase 10 complete (exit note: `docs/plan/phase-exits/p10.md`). Three features
were planned behind measured gates; **one shipped, one shipped after its
suite falsified two designs, one was killed by its own gate before any user
saw it.** Both long-standing platform carries closed. That ratio is the
system working, not a shortfall — every verdict is in the bench record.

## Answer mode: ask deep search for the best passage (ADR-29)

`GET /v1/search?q=…&mode=deep&fetch_budget=N&answer=true` switches the fetch
selector to the single-best objective — the Weitzman/Pandora regime the VoI
work explicitly reserved for this — reads the most promising pages, and
attaches a `best_passage` block: the best (query, passage) pair among the
pages actually read, verbatim, with its source URL and raw cross-encoder
score. Suite-18 replay: **+10.7pp answer hit-rate over not fetching; 3.8×
the page-selector's hit-rate at fewer fetches.**

Honesty surface: extractive only (never generated, never stitched);
`ce_score` is relevance, NOT correctness — the block is "the best place to
start reading", not "the answer"; an empty-handed run says
`degraded: ["answer_unavailable"]`. Privacy: rides the `fetch_budget` opt-in
egress surface byte-for-byte — no new disclosure class. Answer mode has its
OWN latency budget row instead of silently busting the deep 2.5s budget —
and that row was RE-DERIVED by measurement at the exit, per the project's
own rule for missed estimates: the a-priori 3.0s guess measured 3224ms p50
on-device (the per-fetch passage re-scoring that makes the selector honest
costs two CE batches), so the row ships at the measured ≤3.5s with the
optimization study carried, not silently absorbed (deep itself re-checked
at 2173ms ≤ 2.5s, untouched).

## Trends that see a story building (ADR-28)

`top_movers[]` gains `burst {active, onset_day, days_active}`: a two-state
burst decode that answers what the latest-day z statistically cannot — "is
this series inside a SUSTAINED elevation, and since when". A story building
over 3–5 days never makes any single day extreme; suite 17 measured the
shipped decoder at ramp TPR 0.69 vs the incumbent's 0.41 (tuning) and +62%
relative on an overdispersed hold-out, at LOWER false-positive rate than
the incumbent on both. The suite falsified two estimator designs before
passing; the bench doc records all three.

## What the gates killed (recorded, not hidden)

- **Conformal confidence bands (ADR-27) — withdrawn before shipping.** The
  held-out query-style variant measured a 19pp absolute-coverage collapse in
  the same corpus and pipeline; only the relative lift survives, which is
  what the raw `confidence` block already ships. Risk #24's tripwire fired
  exactly as registered.
- **VoI embedding-coverage term — the P9 carry closes with a measured no.**
  The signal is real (potion separates paraphrase registers at 0.716 cosine
  vs 0.052 cross-field) but every swept value model trades more page quality
  than the saved fetches earn. The study suite is the standing judge for any
  future candidate.

## Platform carries closed

- **amd64 restored** (suspended at v0.2.0): native-gnu binary on distroless
  cc — full numkong SIMD dispatch, never the portable fallback (a recall
  defect) — stitched with the arm64 scratch image into one multi-arch
  manifest. An amd64 boot smoke now runs on every PR.
- **1M ANN re-baseline** (P7 carry): recall@10 0.98 at ef=128, p99 1.7ms
  (gate <40ms), 506MB resident — the Profile-F math moves from extrapolated
  to measured. Knob re-derived: Profile F should set
  `vector.expansion_search = 128` (ef=64 decays to 0.94 recall at 1M).
