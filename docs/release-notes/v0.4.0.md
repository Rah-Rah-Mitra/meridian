# Meridian v0.4.0 — the adaptive frontier release

Phase 9 complete (exit note: `docs/plan/phase-exits/p9.md`). Meridian's
routing and reading now adapt — with every adaptive piece behind a measured
gate, an honesty surface, or both. Nothing in this release changes behavior
unless the operator (or the request) asks it to.

## Deep mode can now read the pages — and says why it stopped

`GET /v1/search?q=…&mode=deep&fetch_budget=N` lets one request fetch up to N
result pages (cap `search.deep_fetch_max`, default 2) and re-score them on
their **full text** instead of their snippets. Which pages get fetched is
decided by a value-of-information selector validated on its own benchmark
(suite 15: equal quality to fetch-everything at 30.5% fewer fetches, while
covering MORE distinct evidence clusters than naive top-k fetching — the
novelty term prices near-duplicates of already-read pages out).

The response carries an `analysis` block: `fetches_made`,
`search_stopped_because` (`value_below_reservation` — reading more is
expected to be a net loss — `budget_exhausted`, `deadline`, `exhausted`),
and `estimated_marginal_gain_remaining`. The engine tells you what it left
on the table.

**Privacy, said plainly** (privacy.md): with `fetch_budget` set, the node
makes HTTP requests to result domains, attributable to your direct IP,
triggered by your query. Opt-in per request, direct lane only, never with
`compare`, never persisted — a search cannot ingest what it read.

## Offline policy evaluation, end to end

- The ADR-24 decision log (shipped dark in v0.3.0) gains its full operator
  surface: status, wipe, and `GET /v1/decision-log/ope` — the ADR-25 ship
  gate as an endpoint. It trains the candidate routing policy on the older
  80% of the log and reports the doubly-robust uplift CI on the held-out
  20%, with the verdict spelled out: `pass` only when the 95% CI clears
  zero from above on ≥10k decisions; `inconclusive` means ε-greedy stays.
- The linear Thompson-sampling contextual policy is implemented and **off**
  (`searx.contextual_policy`): it routes nothing until the gate says pass —
  and the gate cannot say pass until real decisions accrue. No silent limbo:
  the verdict will be recorded either way.
- The privacy smoke now exercises the decision log with REAL rows and
  sweeps them for canaries; 20 hermetic invariants pin the lane firewalls,
  including "no query text in the log's raw bytes."

## Operator notes

- New: `fetch_budget` request param; `search.deep_fetch_max` (2),
  `search.deep_fetch_deadline_ms` (1200); `searx.contextual_policy`
  (default false — leave it off until `/v1/decision-log/ope` reports
  `pass`).
- Enabling `searx.decision_log` starts the ADR-25 decision accrual; rows
  are 13 coarse bytes each, 30-day TTL, 20 MB cap, wipe endpoint.
- API evolution remains additive-only (ADR-20); the `analysis` block
  carries `schema: 1`.
