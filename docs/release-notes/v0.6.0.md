# Meridian v0.6.0 — the carry-closure release

Not a phase — a sprint that closed every actionable carry left by the
v0.5.0 exit, each through its own gate, with the negative findings recorded
alongside the wins. Full bench records in `docs/plan/bench/`.

## diversity=evidence: the MMR replacement, and it dominates (PR #26)

`GET /v1/search?q=…&diversity=evidence` reorders the head so each ADR-18
evidence cluster's **canonical** document leads and its syndicated copies
defer to the back. Canonical = the most-shingled member — the superset the
copies derive from: a 75% truncation can outscore its original in BM25, but
it cannot out-shingle it.

Judged by the same dup harness and the same two gates that withdrew
`diversity=mmr` (suite 13b): **alpha-nDCG@10 +0.19/+0.23 AND plain nDCG@10
+0.14/+0.20** (tuning/hold-out) — diversity and relevance stop being a
tradeoff when the demoted thing is genuinely redundant AND genuinely worse.
Two failures are in the record: v1 of this rule ("best-ranked member keeps
the slot") crowned the truncated copies and measured nDCG −13%, and the
harness's first run gated an empty index as PASS (it now refuses).

Per-result `evidence.canonical` annotation ships with it (evidence block
`schema: 2`). Pure local reordering: no privacy surface, no new config;
unsketched (web) results never move — independence is never guessed.

## Answer mode is ~700ms faster, and the 3.0s budget row is won back (PR #28)

`search.answer_passage_cap` (new knob) defaults 16 → 8: the per-fetch
passage-CE batch is the dominant answer-mode latency term, and the device
study measured **p50 2502ms at cap 8** (vs 3196/3224 at cap 16) with 8/9
winning passages living in the first 8 (extraction leads with main
content). The interim ≤3.5s budget re-derivation from the v0.5.0 exit
stands in the record as the honest path; the row is back at ≤3.0s with
~17% headroom. Raise the cap to trade latency for deeper-in-page passages.

## A finding that closed opposite to its premise (PR #27)

The "hybrid-nDCG understated by the broken dense lane" pending item closed
with the opposite finding: hybrid leads BM25 by +0.85pp on the healed lane,
because known-item queries (sampled body words) are BM25's home turf by
construction. The dense lane is provably healthy (1M recall 0.98); the two
historical numbers were never comparable. Recorded, not spun.
